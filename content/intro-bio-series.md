These are my personal introductory biology notes taken at my time at RPI, covering the essence of biology.

> **Note:** These guides are undergoing a major overhaul to improve its quality, readability, and content. For more information, see the [relevant issue on Codeberg](https://codeberg.org/songtech-0912/website/issues/4).

<!-- more -->

- [Evolution](@/evolution.md): a college-level guide to evolutionary theory, its evidence, and its implications.
- [Genetics & medicine](@/genetics-medicine.md): a guide to the basics of genetics and medical science, including DNA, epigenetics, cellular processes, and their role in diseases.
- [Ecology](@/ecology.md): an introductory overview of natural systems and environmental science. Topics covered include ecological systems and habitats, the effects of climates, and the consequences of human interference in the environment.
