Introductory biology series

These are my personal introductory biology notes taken at my time at RPI, covering the essence of biology.

Note: These guides are undergoing a major overhaul to improve its quality, readability, and content. For more information, see the relevant issue on Codeberg.

  • Evolution: a college-level guide to evolutionary theory, its evidence, and its implications.
  • Genetics & medicine: a guide to the basics of genetics and medical science, including DNA, epigenetics, cellular processes, and their role in diseases.
  • Ecology: an introductory overview of natural systems and environmental science. Topics covered include ecological systems and habitats, the effects of climates, and the consequences of human interference in the environment.
